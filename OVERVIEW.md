# Real Estate Diagnostic Report - Product Overview

## Goal

Build a system where a user inputs an address and receives a **comprehensive diagnostic report** that helps them decide whether to buy, avoid, or pursue alternatives.

---

## Core Value Proposition

This product is not just a price estimator.

It is a:

> **Decision engine for real estate purchases**

Combining:

* Machine learning predictions
* Market analytics
* Risk modeling
* Financial breakdowns
* Actionable recommendations

---

## User Flow

1. User inputs address
2. System enriches property + market data
3. Models generate predictions and scores
4. Report is assembled
5. User receives full diagnostic output

---

## Report Sections

### 1. Property Snapshot

Basic property details:

* Beds, baths, sqft, lot size
* Year built
* Last sale price
* Estimated value

---

### 2. Valuation Diagnostic

* Predicted fair market value
* Overpriced / underpriced signal
* Comparable sales
* Price per sqft analysis
* Key valuation drivers

---

### 3. Market Timing Analysis

Answers:

> “Is this a good time to buy?”

Includes:

* Price momentum (3–12 months)
* Inventory trends
* Days on market trends
* Buyer vs seller conditions

---

### 4. True Monthly Cost

Full ownership cost breakdown:

* Mortgage
* Property taxes
* Insurance (critical in Florida)
* HOA
* Maintenance reserve

Output:

> True monthly cost (not just mortgage)

---

### 5. Climate & Insurance Risk

* Flood risk
* Hurricane exposure
* Insurance cost trends
* Long-term risk outlook

Output:

> Forward-looking risk assessment and cost pressure

---

### 6. Hidden Gem Alternatives

If property is overpriced:

* Find similar homes nearby
* Identify underpriced alternatives
* Rank based on similarity + value

---

### 7. Negotiation Strategy

* Suggested offer range
* Market leverage indicators
* Days on market insights
* Price drop history

---

### 8. Liquidity / Exit Risk

* How easy it will be to sell later
* Demand trends
* Days on market comparison

---

### 9. Red Flags

* Key risks
* Cost traps
* Structural / market concerns

---

### 10. Final Summary

Plain-English recommendation:

> Buy / Avoid / Consider alternatives

---

## Long-Term Vision
